# Constants

## `PI`

The mathematical constant $\pi=3.14159...$, to available precision. It is the ratio of a circle's circumference to its diameter. 

## `E`

The mathematical constant $e=\lim\limits_{n\to\infty}\left(1+\frac{1}{n}\right)^n=2.71828...$, to available precision. It is the base of the natural logarithm and exponential function.

## `PHI` and `GOLDEN_RATIO`

The mathematical constant $\varphi=\frac{1+\sqrt{5}}{2}=1.61803...$, to available precision. It denotes the golden ratio and satisfies the quadratic equation $\varphi^2=\varphi+1$.

## `TAU`

The mathematical constant $\tau=2\pi=6.28318...$, to available precision. It is the ratio of a circle's circumference to its radius. 

## `SILVER_RATIO`

The mathematical constant $\sigma=1+\sqrt{2}=...$, to available precision. It is the positive solution of the equation $x^2=2x+1$.

## `SQRT2`

The Pythagoras' constant approximately equal to $\sqrt{2}=1.41421...$, to available precision. 

## `SQRT3`

The constant approximately equal to $\sqrt{3}=1.73205...$, to available precision. 

## `SQRT5`

The constant approximately equal to $\sqrt{5}=2.23606...$, to available precision. 

## `NaN`

The constant, standing for *Not a Number*, is a particular value of a  floating-point number data type which is undefined as a number, such as the result of $\frac{\infty}{\infty}$ or $\frac{0}{0}$.

## `INFINITY`

The floating-point constant representing mathematical infinity $\infty$, which can be obtained, e.g., by $\frac{1}{0}$.

## `NEGATIVE_INFINITY`

The floating-point constant representing mathematical negative infinity $-\infty$, which can be obtained, e.g., by $\frac{-1}{0}$.
